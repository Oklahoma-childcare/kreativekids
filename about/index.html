Where little minds

Oklahoma Human Services lists the center as a three-star program offering year-round care, with weekday hours of 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Care age groups in the state listing include infants, toddlers, preschoolers, and school-age children. The listing also indicates participation in childcare subsidy assistance.

These are state-listed details, not a promise of currently available spaces. Please call the center for up-to-date enrollment information.
